Company-Specific AI Skill Packs

A versioned skill pack for one agreed workflow, with instructions, examples, tests, and operating guidance.

Discuss this service →Compare services

The problem this addresses

Useful prompts live in individual chats. Reviewers cannot consistently reproduce the work or tell which sources and rules shaped an answer.

Who it is for: Content operations, enablement, and technical teams.

What you receive

  • A workflow brief covering audience, inputs, outputs, decision rights, and escalation
  • Structured instructions in Markdown with source-use rules and handling of unknowns
  • Output templates, terminology guidance, and approved examples
  • Representative test cases, scoring criteria, and recorded limitations
  • An installation or adoption guide for the agreed environment, plus a team walkthrough

How the work runs

  1. Capture

    Interview the workflow owner and gather approved instructions, examples, and common failures.

  2. Package

    Organize the expertise into a maintainable instruction set, templates, and evidence rules.

  3. Evaluate

    Test representative and exception cases, inspect unsupported claims, and revise the pack.

  4. Enable

    Hand over the versioned files and train the owner to update and retest them.

A concrete example

A documentation team turns its source-packet rules, procedure template, editorial standard, and technical review requirements into a reusable drafting skill.

Illustrative scenario; not a client outcome or a measured improvement claim.

How progress is assessed

Task completion, evidence support, reviewer corrections, repeatability, and maintenance effort on comparable cases.

Agree the baseline, sample, success conditions, and evidence with the accountable owners before evaluating results.

Scope and commercial model

Fixed-scope project. Scoped quote. Agree the use case, source sample, tool access, implementation responsibilities, reviewers, timeline, and fee before work begins.

Work is bounded to the agreed environment and deliverables. Production changes and substantive policy or content approvals remain with the named customer owners.
