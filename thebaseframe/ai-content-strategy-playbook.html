Practical playbook · Four steps

AI Content Strategy: from problem to artifact

A source-grounded workflow brief and a reviewed content artifact.

  1. 1. Choose one workflow

    Select one audience and task. Compare outlining, drafting, editing, and maintenance; choose a task with approved evidence and a clear reviewer.

    Check before continuing: Record evidence, an accountable owner, and any unresolved assumptions.

  2. 2. Model the content

    Record prerequisites, steps, reference fields, source IDs, version, audience, and ownership. Separate reusable facts from task-specific instructions.

    Check before continuing: Record evidence, an accountable owner, and any unresolved assumptions.

  3. 3. Draft with evidence

    Supply a bounded source packet, desired structure, and rules for unknowns. Preserve the prompt and output, then map material claims to sources.

    Check before continuing: Record evidence, an accountable owner, and any unresolved assumptions.

  4. 4. Review and maintain

    Verify commands and meaning, resolve unknowns, and record approval. Define which source changes trigger a new review.

    Check before continuing: Record evidence, an accountable owner, and any unresolved assumptions.

Review your completed work

  • Can another person trace the decision to approved evidence?
  • Are ownership, version, permissions, and limitations visible?
  • Have you separated an illustrative example from a measured result?
  • Is there a clear next action for unresolved issues?
