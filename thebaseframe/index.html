The problem

AI use is spreading. A shared way of working often isn’t.

One person has a useful prompt. Another has a different answer. The source is hard to find, and a reviewer still has to reconstruct how the work was done.

01 · Scattered knowledge

Which answer should we trust?

Duplicate guides, unclear owners, and old versions make every answer harder to verify.

02 · Individual habits

The process lives with one person.

Prompts and decisions stay in personal chats. Other people cannot reliably repeat the work.

03 · Repeated checking

Review becomes a second production line.

Teams spend time finding evidence, correcting claims, and resolving questions that should have been caught earlier.

The recommendation

Connect the task, the knowledge, and the decision.

Choose one recurring workflow. Establish its approved sources, define where AI helps, and make quality checks and ownership part of the process.

  2. PrepareSources, structure, and permissions.
