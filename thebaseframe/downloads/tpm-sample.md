# TPM Agent Kit: free sample

All scenario details are fictional.

## Problem
A knowledge platform launch depends on content migration, permissions testing, and support training. Migration is on track, but the permissions owner has no test evidence.

## Task
Write a status update, the decision needed, and a launch condition. Assign a decision owner and date without inventing completion.

## Worked response
Report the permissions evidence gap as a launch risk. Assign an owner to provide test results by a named decision date. Tie launch to reviewed access-control evidence, migration validation, and training readiness. An agent may draft the update; accountable owners verify status and approve launch.
