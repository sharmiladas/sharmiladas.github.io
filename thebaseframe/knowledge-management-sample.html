Free sample · 15–20 minutes

Knowledge Management: try the method

Work through this fictional scenario. Write your answer before opening the worked response.

The problem

Support teams maintain three answers to the same access question. One is current, one is outdated, and one has no owner. Search users cannot tell which answer to trust.

Your task

Define the authoritative answer, required metadata, a reuse-and-improve workflow, and an escalation path when sources disagree.

Worked response and reasoning

Have the domain owner resolve the substantive answer. Merge or retire duplicates with traceable redirects where supported. Record owner, audience, task, version, permissions, and review date. Let stewards improve routine structure; send policy changes to the domain owner. Review repeated searches and support questions to guide improvements.
