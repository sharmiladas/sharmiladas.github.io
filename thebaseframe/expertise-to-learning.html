Expertise-to-Learning Service

A practical learning module and reusable playbook grounded in approved expertise.

Discuss this service →Compare services

The problem this addresses

Important expertise is spread across documents and experienced people. New team members struggle to apply it consistently.

Who it is for: Enablement, training, and professional-services teams.

What you receive

  • An audience and task analysis with learning objectives
  • A source map and expert-review plan
  • An agreed module with worked examples, exercises, and knowledge checks
  • A reusable workplace artifact and facilitator or self-study guide
  • A pilot feedback summary, revision log, and maintenance handoff

How the work runs

  1. Discover

    Choose one capability and gather approved sources and expert input.

  2. Design

    Map what learners need to do to objectives, examples, practice, and an applied artifact.

  3. Build

    Create the agreed learning materials and review substantive content with the expert owner.

  4. Pilot

    Collect learner feedback and assess artifact quality, then revise and hand over.

A concrete example

A support team turns an escalation playbook into a lesson, realistic cases, a decision exercise, and a reusable checklist reviewed by its subject-matter experts.

Illustrative scenario; not a client outcome or a measured improvement claim.

How progress is assessed

Exercise performance, quality of the completed artifact, expert corrections, learner feedback, and subsequent use in work.

Agree the baseline, sample, success conditions, and evidence with the accountable owners before evaluating results.

Scope and commercial model

Fixed-scope project. Scoped quote. Agree the use case, source sample, tool access, implementation responsibilities, reviewers, timeline, and fee before work begins.

Work is bounded to the agreed environment and deliverables. Production changes and substantive policy or content approvals remain with the named customer owners.
