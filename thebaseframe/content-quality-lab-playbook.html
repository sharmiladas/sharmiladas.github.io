Practical playbook · Four steps

AI Content Quality Lab: from problem to artifact

An evaluation scorecard, failure backlog, and pilot decision memo.

  1. 1. Prepare sources and cases

    Record source authority, version, scope, and permissions. Build representative questions and expected behavior with held-out cases.

    Check before continuing: Record evidence, an accountable owner, and any unresolved assumptions.

  2. 2. Calibrate reviewers

    Score accuracy, grounding, relevance, usefulness, and safety independently. Discuss disagreements using evidence and preserve original ratings.

    Check before continuing: Record evidence, an accountable owner, and any unresolved assumptions.

  3. 3. Compare controlled experiments

    Record prompts, corpus, model, and retrieval settings. Separate hand-authored fixtures from actual model responses and leave unmeasured values blank.

    Check before continuing: Record evidence, an accountable owner, and any unresolved assumptions.

  4. 4. Make a bounded decision

    Review critical failures separately from averages. Record conditions, accountable owners, monitoring, and rollback before a limited pilot.

    Check before continuing: Record evidence, an accountable owner, and any unresolved assumptions.

Review your completed work

  • Can another person trace the decision to approved evidence?
  • Are ownership, version, permissions, and limitations visible?
  • Have you separated an illustrative example from a measured result?
  • Is there a clear next action for unresolved issues?
