Free sample · 15–20 minutes

AI Content Strategy: try the method

Work through this fictional scenario. Write your answer before opening the worked response.

The problem

A team wants AI to draft an API setup guide. The approved source lists authentication and a create operation, but says nothing about rate limits or automatic retries.

Your task

Write a source packet and drafting instruction. Identify what may be drafted, which details must stay unknown, and who must review the result.

Worked response and reasoning

Allow drafting only from the approved source. Ask for claim-to-source references and an unknowns list. Leave rate limits and retry behavior unresolved until the product owner supplies evidence. Have a technical reviewer validate commands before publication.
