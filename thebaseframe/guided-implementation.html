Good starting points

A source-linked support response

Retrieve approved answers, draft within the evidence, and route unanswered questions for review.

A documentation change workflow

A knowledge maintenance queue

A program reporting workflow

Assemble owner-verified milestones, risks, and decisions into a consistent update.

What the engagement produces

  1. 1. Workflow and baseline

    One audience, one recurring task, current effort and failure points, and agreed success measures.

  2. 2. Prepared source set

    Approved sources, authority, versions, permissions, and a plan for resolving missing evidence.

  3. 3. A bounded pilot workflow

    An agreed implementation with review steps, test cases, and a record of results and limitations.

  4. 4. Operating handoff

    Run instructions, named owners, maintenance triggers, escalation, monitoring, and rollback responsibilities.

  5. 5. Follow-up review

    Review observed use, remaining failures, and whether to refine, continue, or expand the pilot.

Measure before claiming improvement

MeasureWhat to record
Time per taskComparable tasks before and during the pilot, including review and rework.
QualitySource-grounded correctness, completion, and critical failures.
Review effortTime, corrections, and reasons reviewers return work.
AdoptionEligible users and tasks, actual use, and reasons for abandonment.

Scope and pricing

Scoped quote. Agree the workflow, systems, integrations, implementation responsibilities, success conditions, timeline, and fee before work begins. Engineering or security participation is defined when scoping the pilot.

A production rollout, additional integrations, and continued operational support require an explicit scope. The follow-up review is included in the agreed engagement.
