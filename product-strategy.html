Expertise · Product strategy

Product strategy for knowledge and content platforms.

I connect customer needs, platform capabilities, and business priorities to a product direction teams can deliver and measure.

Define the problem

Translate customer journeys and content audits into requirements for authoring, discovery, reuse, and self-service.

Choose where to invest

Evaluate build versus buy, time to value, extensibility, maintenance, risk, and opportunity cost.

Shape the roadmap

Turn platform choices into phased capabilities, with owners, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and launch decisions.

Measure usefulness

Use quality, review time, suggestion accuracy, reuse, and customer outcomes to assess whether the product solves the intended problem.
