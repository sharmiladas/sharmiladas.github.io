Multi-Year Strategy

From content factory to intelligent knowledge system

A three-year strategy I drove for a global customer-operations environment: move from fragmented pages and reactive publishing toward structured, reusable, contextual knowledge that can power self-service, support, automation, and AI.

YEAR 1

Foundation

Establish ownership, governance, taxonomy, content models, lifecycle controls, quality standards, and a shared source of truth.

  • Inventory and rationalize
  • Define content types and metadata
  • Clarify ownership and publishing controls
  • Instrument quality and findability
YEAR 2

Reuse & orchestration

Separate knowledge from presentation so authoritative components can be reused across journeys, channels, locales, and experiences.

  • Modular structured content
  • Cross-channel reuse
  • Workflow automation
  • API-ready knowledge delivery
YEAR 3

Intelligence

Use governed knowledge to support contextual delivery, personalization, automation, and AI-assisted experiences with measurable quality.

  • Context-aware delivery
  • AI-assisted retrieval & generation
  • Evaluation and feedback loops
  • Continuous learning from performance

Point of View

AI content strategy is not a prompting strategy.

The durable advantage is the knowledge layer: structured, governed, retrievable, measurable context that allows AI systems to make better decisions.

AI should automate transformation and evaluation—not outsource strategy. Creative judgment, positioning, risk decisions, and differentiated ideas remain human responsibilities.
