FORMA 2.0 · TWICE A WEEK

Your week of movement.

Move every day, vary the work.

Yoga + dance daily. Full-body resistance twice a week. Cardio twice a week. Keep the other days light.

Cardio, twice a week

Twenty minutes of easy walking, low-impact step-touch, and a gentle finish. You can substitute a brisk walk or your usual cardio class and use the plan as a guide.

These two sessions are a starting plan. They do not by themselves meet the general 150-minute weekly moderate-activity guideline. Read the activity guidance.

Repeat your selected movements on both days. Each movement has one library entry, even when it appears in several references. Start with the smaller routine and adjust the list as you learn.

Choose resistance exercises to repeat on both strength days. The starter list covers chest, glutes, abs, and arms; yoga and dance live in the daily plan.

About your references

The library combines the glute poster, upper-back stretches, yoga pose chart, and identifiable movements from the arm, wall-sit, Pilates, and moon-salutation clips.
