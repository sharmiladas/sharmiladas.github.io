AI + CONTENT · 2026

When Content Gets Cheap, Decisions Get Expensive

Generation is becoming abundant. The strategic scarcity moves to judgment: what is true, what is approved, what matters, and what should ship.

The economics changed

When the cost of producing a plausible first draft falls toward zero, volume stops being a useful proxy for capability. The differentiator becomes the decision system around generation: source authority, messaging hierarchy, evidence, policy, evaluation, and the ability to know when a human must decide.

Content strategy moves upstream

The highest-value content strategist is no longer only shaping the artifact. The role increasingly defines the knowledge the system can use, the rules it must respect, the quality bar it must pass, and the feedback that improves the system over time.

The new bottleneck is judgment

Organizations will not win by generating the most variants. They will win by making better decisions faster—with a traceable connection between business intent, trusted knowledge, output quality, and performance.

AI content strategy is the design of the decision system around generation.
KNOWLEDGE → RETRIEVAL → CONTEXT → GENERATION → EVALUATION → GOVERNANCE → LEARNING
