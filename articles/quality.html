Why AI Quality Should Start Before Review

Quality is not a final editorial checkpoint. It is a property of the entire system.

Review is too late

A reviewer can catch a bad claim after generation, but that does not repair the stale source, weak retrieval, missing policy, or ambiguous message framework that caused it.

Design quality into every layer

Knowledge should have ownership and authority. Retrieval should be evaluated. Context should be minimal and high-signal. Generation should operate inside explicit constraints. Output should pass deterministic and model-based gates before risk-based human review.

Measure what matters

Useful rubrics go beyond grammar: groundedness, factual accuracy, audience relevance, strategic fidelity, brand fidelity, message consistency, policy compliance, clarity, and value.

AI content strategy is the design of the decision system around generation.
KNOWLEDGE → RETRIEVAL → CONTEXT → GENERATION → EVALUATION → GOVERNANCE → LEARNING
