The Enterprise AI Problem Hiding in Your Content

Before blaming the model, inspect the knowledge it is being asked to reason over.

AI exposes old content debt

Duplicate pages, conflicting claims, weak ownership, stale guidance, missing metadata, and ambiguous terminology were manageable when humans navigated around them. Retrieval systems surface those inconsistencies at machine speed.

The knowledge layer is infrastructure

Enterprise AI needs more than a vector database. It needs authoritative sources, structured entities and relationships, lifecycle rules, versioning, access controls, and metadata that expresses audience, market, product, risk, and intent.

Fix the source, not only the answer

A durable AI program creates a feedback loop from output failures back to the broken layer. Sometimes the prompt is wrong. Often the knowledge, retrieval, or governance is.

AI content strategy is the design of the decision system around generation.
KNOWLEDGE → RETRIEVAL → CONTEXT → GENERATION → EVALUATION → GOVERNANCE → LEARNING
